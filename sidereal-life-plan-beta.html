Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use tropical astrology?

No. We use sidereal/Vedic astrology with the Lahiri ayanamsa and Whole Sign houses, calculated via Swiss Ephemeris.

Is this AI generated?

Yes, but grounded in precise chart math and a clear method. The tool handles the pattern analysis; a person reviews and edits every report during beta before it's sent to you.

Will you tell me my future?

No predictions. We focus on timing, patterns, and coaching, so you can design your life and test how your chart's tendencies actually show up for you.

What if my birth time is off?

We'll flag exactly what that affects — typically finer details like your exact Ascendant degree or Antardasha entry dates — and what stays stable regardless, like your Moon sign and broader dasha periods.

How is this different from astrology apps?

Depth, method transparency, and structure. This isn't daily content — it's a one-time, in-depth report built around experiments and decision-making, not a feed.
