Frequently asked questions

Is this tropical astrology?

No. This is sidereal (Vedic), with the Lahiri ayanamsa and Whole Sign houses, calculated from real ephemeris data.

Is this AI generated?

The math is machine-precise and cross-checked; the writing is personally reviewed and edited before anything is sent. During beta, every report gets a human pass.

Will you tell me my future?

No predictions. This is about timing, patterns, and decisions, so you can test how your chart's tendencies actually show up in your life.

What if my birth time is off?

The report flags exactly what that affects (fine details like your exact Ascendant degree) and what stays solid regardless (Moon sign, broader dasha periods).
